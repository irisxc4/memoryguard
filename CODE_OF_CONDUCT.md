# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in MemoryGuard open and welcoming to everyone,
regardless of background or identity. We expect participants to act
professionally, respectfully, and in good faith.

## Expected behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Keep discussions, issues, and reviews relevant and actionable.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
deliberate intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, publishing private
information, and other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional
setting.

## Enforcement

For a non-sensitive conduct concern, use [GitHub Discussions][discussions]. Do
not include sensitive personal information in a public issue or discussion.
For sensitive details, use the repository's private GitHub contact channel
first and request a confidential thread. Maintainers will review reports
fairly, protect confidentiality where possible, and may remove content or
restrict participation when needed.

This code of conduct applies in project spaces and whenever an individual is
representing the project.

## Scope

Maintainers may clarify or update this code of conduct as the project evolves.
Participants who do not follow it may be asked to stop the behavior or leave
the project space.
