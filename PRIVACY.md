# MemoryGuard Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-31

MemoryGuard is open-source local software and a skills-only plugin. This policy
describes the MemoryGuard repository and its local MCP server; it does not
replace the policies of the coding-agent host, PyPI, GitHub, OpenAI, or any
other provider you choose to use.

## What MemoryGuard does

MemoryGuard does not operate a hosted MemoryGuard service, require a
MemoryGuard account, or run a MemoryGuard telemetry backend. Depending on your
configuration, it stores governed memory, rules, evidence, history, indexes,
and optional usage summaries on your machine. The exact files and locations
are controlled by the selected workspace and MemoryGuard configuration.

MemoryGuard does not send data to a remote service by default. Local data stays
under paths controlled by you unless you explicitly configure a host or
third-party integration. A remote model, embedding provider, or another
third-party integration can receive data only when you explicitly configure and
authorize that operation. That provider's own privacy policy and terms then
apply.

## Your responsibilities

Review the filesystem permissions, authorized source folders, host settings,
and third-party integrations used with MemoryGuard. Keep credentials, API
keys, and other secrets out of public issues, support requests, prompts,
screenshots, and logs. The GitHub issue tracker is a public support channel;
share a sanitized reproduction instead.

## Changes and contact

This policy may be updated with the repository. For privacy questions or to
report a concern, open a sanitized issue at
<https://github.com/irisxc4/memoryguard/issues>.
